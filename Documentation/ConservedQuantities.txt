Summary of the magnitudes saved in "ConservedQuantities.txt"
 (Tue Jul  4 11:37:37 CEST 2017)


1st column:
  Number of iterations

2nd column:
  Total energy of the system computed as the sum of the electric energy (using 
  the electric field on the nodes), the magnetic energy (using the magnetic 
  field on the nodes) and the kinetic energy from the particles. For more 
  details see columns 4,5,6.

3rd column:
  Variation of the total energy (using as a reference the energy of the system 
  after the first iteration).

4th column:
  Electric energy of the system. This value is computed as the sum over all 
  the nodes (except from the last face of nodes in each directions) of the 
  square of the electric field  divided by 8 pi and multiply by the volume of 
  the nodes.
         1/2 1/(4 pi) E E vol
  The electric field defined on the nodes is used. 

5th column: 
  Magnetic energy of the system. This value is computed as the sum over all
  the cells of the square of the magnetic field divided by 8 pi and multiply by 
  the volume of the cells. 
         1/2 1/(4 pi vol) B B 
  The magnetic field defined on the centers is used. It only uses the magnetic 
  field created by the particles (the external field is not included).

6th column:
  Kinetic energy of the particles. It is the sum over all the particles of 
  one half of the velocity square multiply by its mass.
         1/2 m v v 
 
7th and 8th column:
  Total number of particles of and total charge of particles of the first
  specie defined in the input file

(9th and 10th column ...):
  Total number of particles of and total charge of particles of the nth
  specie defined in the input file
